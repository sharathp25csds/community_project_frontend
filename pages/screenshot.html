Recommended Safety Actions
  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, or banking credentials with anyone.
  • Do not click links or install apps (e.g., APK files, AnyDesk, TeamViewer) sent via SMS or chat.
  • Verify unexpected demands (electricity cut, KYC expiry, lottery) directly through official bank/utility websites or customer care numbers.
  • If you have already transferred funds, immediately dial 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) and report to your bank.
